Mission Briefing

🎅 Your Mission

The holiday celebration has been locked behind a series of puzzles.

Your team must solve each challenge, pay attention to the clues, and work together to reach the final answer.

Can your team escape before time runs out?

Before You Begin

📜 Escape Room Rules

  1. Work with your assigned team.
  2. Everyone should contribute ideas.
  3. Read each clue carefully before answering.
  4. Keep answers with your team until asked to share.
  5. Be respectful and let everyone participate.
  6. No searching online for puzzle answers.
  7. Have fun and keep the holiday spirit alive!
