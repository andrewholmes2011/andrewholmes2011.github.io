Quick answers

Common computer issues

Simple, safe steps for solving everyday computer and technology problems.

Everyday fixes

My computer is slow. What should I try?

Restart it, close unused apps and tabs, check available storage, and install system updates. Ask a trusted adult or technician before deleting anything important.

My Wi-Fi is not working.

Check other devices, move closer to the router, restart the router and device, and verify Wi-Fi is enabled. If everything is offline, check for an outage.

An app froze. How do I fix it?

Wait briefly, close and reopen it, then use the operating system’s task manager if needed. Restart the computer and check for app updates.

My computer will not turn on.

Check the charger and outlet, disconnect accessories, and try holding the power button. Stop and get help if there is heat, smoke, or a damaged battery.

Accounts & security

How do I make an account safer?

Use a unique password, enable multi-factor authentication, keep recovery details current, and never share verification codes.

Why should I install updates?

Updates improve stability and fix security weaknesses. Use official update tools and keep your operating system, browser, and important apps current.

What should I do about a suspicious email?

Do not click links or open attachments. Check the sender, report or delete the message, and contact the organization through a trusted channel.

What is a backup?

A backup is an extra copy of important data stored separately from the original. Keep more than one copy and test that files can be restored.
