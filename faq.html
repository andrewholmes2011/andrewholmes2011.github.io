Common computer issues

Simple, safe steps for solving everyday computer and technology problems.

Everyday fixes

My computer is slow. What should I try?

Restart it, close unused apps and browser tabs, check available storage, and install pending system updates. If it is still slow, check for unwanted programs and ask a trusted adult or technician before deleting anything important.

My Wi-Fi is not working.

Check whether other devices connect, move closer to the router, restart the router and your device, and verify Wi-Fi is enabled. If every device is offline, the internet provider may be having an outage.

An app froze. How do I fix it?

Wait briefly, then close and reopen the app. If it will not close, use the operating system’s task manager or force-quit tool, restart the computer, and check the app for updates.

My computer will not turn on.

Check the power cable or charger, try another outlet, disconnect accessories, and hold the power button for a few seconds. For a laptop, let it charge before trying again. Get help if you notice heat, smoke, or a damaged battery.

Accounts & security

How do I make an account safer?

Use a unique password, turn on multi-factor authentication, keep recovery details current, and never share verification codes.

Why should I install updates?

Updates improve stability and fix security weaknesses. Use official update tools and keep your operating system, browser, and important apps current.

What should I do about a suspicious email?

Do not click links or open attachments. Check the sender carefully, report or delete the message, and contact the organization through a trusted website or phone number.

What is a backup?

A backup is an extra copy of important data stored separately from the original. Keep more than one copy when possible and test that files can be restored.
